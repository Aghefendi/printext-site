Printext Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Printext ("the app") is made by Mehmet Agâh Çiçek ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Printext on iPhone.

The short version

  • We do not collect any data. Printext has no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Your conversations stay on your iPhone. Importing, reviewing and creating documents all happen on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app uses the network only for App Store purchases, which Apple handles.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

When you create a case, Printext stores on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage:

  • the messages you imported, the participants' names and numbers, and the corrections you made (with their edit log);
  • the source material you chose: Shortcut records, screenshots or frames from a screen recording, or a WhatsApp export and its media;
  • the documents you exported (PDF, CSV, TXT) and a record of each export (date, type and a SHA-256 fingerprint).

These files are protected with iOS Data Protection and are readable only when your iPhone is unlocked. They are part of your device backup (for example iCloud Backup, if you turned it on); that backup is handled by Apple under your Apple Account settings, and we have no access to it.

You can delete a case in the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Messages

Apps on iPhone cannot read the Messages app. Printext receives messages only when you run the Printext Shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app:

  • You choose the conversation and the period.
  • Shortcuts asks for your permission before it shares messages with Printext.
  • The Shortcut may pass messages from other conversations in the same period. Printext keeps only the conversation you chose; the others are discarded in memory and never saved.

Photos, screen recordings and files

  • Screenshots and screen recordings are chosen with the iOS photo picker. Printext gets only the items you pick and does not ask for access to your photo library.
  • WhatsApp exports and other files are read only when you open them in Printext.
  • Text in images is recognized on your iPhone with Apple's on-device Vision framework.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and processed by Apple. Printext checks your purchases on your device with Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple gives developers sales reports that do not identify you.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive what you choose to send: your email address and your message. When you start an email from Printext, the draft contains only the app version and the iOS version; nothing from your cases. Please do not send conversations or documents unless you want us to see them. We use support emails only to answer you and delete them within 12 months.

Diagnostics

Printext contains no crash-reporting or analytics tools. If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Links

Links in the app (for example this policy, Apple's Terms of Use, or subscription management) open in Safari, Mail or the App Store. Those services follow their own privacy policies.

Children

Printext is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information that a company holds about you. We do not hold any personal information from the app. For support emails, write to us and we will answer your request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of Printext ever collects data, the policy and the App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
