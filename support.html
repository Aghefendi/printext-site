Printext Support

Printext turns a text conversation into a PDF, CSV or TXT file on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Contact: info@hemenhesap.com. We usually answer within two business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version (Printext adds them when you tap Settings > Contact Support). Do not send conversations unless you want us to see them.

Printext formats conversations for court use. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice.

Getting started

  1. Tap New Export.
  2. Choose where the conversation comes from:
    • From Messages (iOS 26 or later, recommended): exact date, time and sender for every message.
    • Screen Recording or Screenshots: works on any iOS version Printext supports, and for photos in a thread.
    • WhatsApp Chat: a file from WhatsApp's Export Chat.
  3. Review the messages, then choose Continue to set up the document.
  4. Preview it, then Export.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I need a Shortcut?

iPhone does not let apps read the Messages app. Apple's Shortcuts app can, with your permission. The Printext Shortcut finds the messages of the period you choose and hands them to Printext. You add it once with one tap: New Export > From Messages > Add Shortcut, then Add Shortcut in Shortcuts.

Is my conversation uploaded?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. Printext has no account and no servers, and it collects no data. The network is used only for App Store purchases.

How far back can I export?

Choose Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or a longer period before you run the Shortcut. Long periods take longer, because the Shortcut goes through every message of that period on your iPhone before Printext keeps the conversation you chose. If a long period is slow, export it in parts, for example one case per month.

What does the free version include?

Unlimited import and review, a full preview with a "PREVIEW" watermark, and exports of up to 20 messages without a watermark.

What do the purchases include?

  • 7-Day Case Pass, $9.99: unlimited exports for 7 days. It does not renew.
  • Pro Annual, $39.99 per year: unlimited exports. Renews automatically until you cancel.
  • Lifetime, $59.99: unlimited exports, paid once.

Prices are in US dollars and vary by country.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings > Restore Purchases in Printext, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel Pro Annual?

On your iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Printext Pro > Cancel Subscription, or in Printext: Settings > Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The 7-Day Case Pass and Lifetime never renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose Request a refund.

Is the PDF accepted in court?

Printext formats conversations for court use: numbered messages, exact times, exhibit labels, Bates numbers, page numbers and an integrity page. Whether a document is accepted is up to the court and your attorney. This is not legal advice.

What does "≈" before a time mean?

The time is approximate. It happens only with screen recordings and screenshots, where some times are worked out from the nearest time shown on screen. Messages imported with the Shortcut always have exact times.

What does "†" next to a message mean?

You corrected that message (sender, text or time). The original is kept in the case, and the integrity page lists every correction with its time.

Which time zone does the document use?

Your iPhone's time zone, unless you choose another one in Document Settings. The cover page names it.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Swipe a case to delete it. Deleting the app deletes everything Printext stored.

Troubleshooting

The Shortcut says it found no messages

  • Check the period: a conversation with no messages in the last 7 days returns nothing for Last 7 days. Try a longer period.
  • Open the Messages app once and make sure the conversation is there. If you use Messages in iCloud, wait until it has finished syncing.
  • Start the Shortcut from Printext (Run Shortcut), not from the Shortcuts app. Run from Shortcuts, it only reminds you to open Printext.

Shortcuts asks "Allow … to share … with Printext?"

That is Apple's privacy question. Choose Allow Once (or Always Allow, so it is not asked again). If you chose Don't Allow, run the Shortcut again; to change a saved answer, open the Shortcut's details in the Shortcuts app and look under Privacy.

"Printext Import" shortcut not found

You may have renamed or deleted it. In Printext, go to Settings > Set Up the Shortcut Again and add it again. Keep the name "Printext Import".

The Shortcut stops with an error

Update to the latest iOS, then add the Shortcut again (Settings > Set Up the Shortcut Again). If it still fails, email us the exact error text; please do not include the messages. Meanwhile, Screen Recording works for any conversation.

Printext is not in WhatsApp's share sheet

In WhatsApp choose Export Chat, then Save to Files. In Printext tap New Export > WhatsApp Chat and open the file from Files.

A screen recording gave wrong or missing messages

  • Scroll slowly and stop briefly every screen.
  • At each stop, swipe left on the conversation to show every message's exact time.
  • Messages you can fix are highlighted; tap Next flagged to go through them.
  • Possible gaps are marked in the document; record that part again as a new case.

The paywall says prices couldn't be loaded

Check your internet connection and tap Try Again. Purchases need the App Store; everything else works offline.

My purchase is "Waiting for approval"

With Ask to Buy, a family organizer approves the purchase. You can export as soon as it is approved.
